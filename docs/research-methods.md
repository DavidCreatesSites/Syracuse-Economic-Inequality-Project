# Research methods

The current methodology and limitations are documented in [the research review](research-review.md) and the website’s [Sources tab](../sources.html#reading-the-data).

Every chart identifies its source, geography, period, units, and review status. Values transcribed from the paper are distinguished from values checked against the source. Approximate Opportunity Atlas ranges retain explicit warnings about missing filters. Association is not presented as causation, and incompatible city, district, county, and metropolitan estimates are not treated as interchangeable.
